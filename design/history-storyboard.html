MotionLog · Signage Sprint · History tab storyboard

Did I do my PT?

History becomes an adherence view: across days, did you finish what your PT prescribed, and did you hit the targets? Five bird's-eye overviews, five ways to show a single day, and two edge states. Every screen uses the same sample data: the four-move routine from Today, logged from Oct 18 to today, Mon Nov 9. Sundays are rest days.

Hit: logged at or above the prescription Short: logged, under the target Skipped: prescribed, never logged

Two separate questions run through every screen. Done means every prescribed move was logged that day. On target means the logged sets, reps or time met the prescription.

Bird's-eye overviews

The History tab's first screen. Each one answers “how have I been doing?” at a different zoom.

Day detail

All five open the same day, Wed · Nov 4, because it has all three outcomes: two moves hit, Heel raise short (2 × 8 of 2 × 12), and Clamshell skipped.

5B · Calendar on top, day drops down

The calendar stays frozen at the top. Tapping a day drops a panel out from under the calendar to fill the lower half. The day's header scrolls with the list. Dragging the grab strip up raises the panel to 75% of the screen height to show more of the day.

Open & switch

  • Tap a past day or today. The panel unrolls downward from the calendar's edge, about 0.45 s.
  • At half height, tapping another day crossfades the contents in place.

Heights

  • Two snap points: half (just under the calendar) and 75% of the screen.
  • Drag the grab strip up more than about 50 pt, or flick up, to reach 75%. Drag down to return to half.
  • The header and list scroll together. Only the grab strip takes the drag.

Close

  • ✕ beside the title, tapping the selected day again, or Esc.
  • Drag the grab strip down from half height. The panel rolls back up under the calendar.
  • VoiceOver: the ✕ button plus the two-finger scrub (escape) gesture.

Row content

  • Status icon, move name, and the prescription only (sets × reps or time, plus band or weight).
  • No outcome labels, no logged values, no notes or voice notes.

Edge states

Days with no logs, and the day that isn't over yet.

How they could combine

The overviews and detail layouts are independent, so any pair works. Three that fit together well:

Suggested starting point

A calendar + 5 peek sheet + 1 checklist

The calendar answers “am I keeping up?”. The sheet answers “what happened that day?” without losing your place. The checklist is the full record and reuses Today's rows.

Closest to the current build

C day feed + 1 checklist

Smallest change from today's HistoryView: the strip gets fractions, rows become days, and the detail is the Today list with Plan and Did lines.

For PT check-ins

D matrix + 2 table

Best for spotting which move keeps slipping and for showing your PT a clean record. Could live behind a “By move” toggle on top of A or C.

Open questions

  • Does a day count as done once every move is logged, or only when all are on target? The screens keep these as two separate measures.
  • Where does a goal come from? E assumes a weekly target that the PT or the user sets.
  • History needs the routine as it was on each date. When the PT changes the plan, past days should still show the old prescription.
  • Per-set logging (layout 3) and back-dated logs (missed day) both need changes to the data model.
